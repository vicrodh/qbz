A native hi‑fi music player for Linux

QBZ plays your Qobuz subscription, your own files and your Plex, Jellyfin or Navidrome server through one bit-perfect pipeline. A Rust engine, a Qt interface, and no browser anywhere in between.

Signal path

What comes in is what goes out.

Every source is decoded by the same engine and handed to your DAC at its native resolution. Nothing in the middle changes the samples.

Input

Sources

Qobuz up to 24 bit / 192 kHz. Local FLAC, ALAC, WavPack, Vorbis, Opus, MP3, AAC, DSF/DFF. Plex, Jellyfin, Subsonic. Audio CD and SACD.

Engine

One Rust process

Symphonia decoders, per-track sample-rate switching, gapless with prefetch and a two-level cache. No EQ, no DSP, by design.

Output

Straight to hardware

ALSA Direct, PipeWire, PulseAudio, JACK. CoreAudio Direct on macOS, WASAPI exclusive on Windows. DSD over DoP or native. Chromecast, DLNA, Qobuz Connect.
